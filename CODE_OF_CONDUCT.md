# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, constructive, and accessible to everyone.

We welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels, including writers, creators, editors, educators, researchers, developers, students, and industry professionals.

Participation should remain safe and productive regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, socioeconomic status, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

* Communicate respectfully
* Critique ideas rather than people
* Give specific and constructive feedback
* Acknowledge uncertainty
* Support claims with examples or evidence where practical
* Respect different writing traditions, genres, cultures, and artistic goals
* Accept reasonable moderation
* Disclose relevant conflicts of interest
* Protect private and confidential material
* Follow copyright and licensing requirements

Disagreement is welcome. Dismissiveness, hostility, and personal attacks are not.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

* Harassment or discrimination
* Personal attacks or insults
* Threats or intimidation
* Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention
* Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior
* Repeatedly derailing discussions
* Publishing private information without permission
* Sharing private scripts or customer material
* Impersonation
* Spam or undisclosed promotion
* Manipulating discussions through coordinated abuse
* Retaliation against someone who reports a concern
* Knowingly making false claims about contributors or their work

## Criticism of Creative Work

This project discusses writing and creative work, which can be personal and subjective.

Feedback should:

* Address the work, rule, example, or result
* Avoid assumptions about the writer
* Avoid presenting taste as fact
* Explain the context in which a criticism applies
* Recognize that conventions vary across genres and cultures
* Avoid using scores to demean or rank individuals

Statements such as “this scoring rule may penalize nonlinear narratives” are appropriate.

Statements attacking a writer’s intelligence, worth, or identity are not.

## Methodology Disagreement

Disagreement about scoring methodology should focus on:

* Definitions
* Evidence
* Examples
* Reproducibility
* Tradeoffs
* Effects on writers
* Possible bias
* Alternative approaches

No contributor is required to accept a claim merely because it comes from a maintainer, expert, organization, or industry professional.

At the same time, disagreement must remain respectful and relevant.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in:

* GitHub Issues
* Pull requests
* Discussions
* Project documentation
* Community meetings
* Project-related chat spaces
* Public interactions representing the project

It may also apply to conduct outside official spaces when that conduct materially affects the safety or participation of project members.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to:

support@loglisted.com

Reports should include, when possible:

* What happened
* Where it happened
* When it happened
* Who was involved
* Relevant links or screenshots
* Whether there is an immediate safety concern

Do not open a public Issue to report sensitive conduct concerns.

## Privacy

Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

Information may be shared with maintainers only when necessary to review the report, protect participants, comply with legal obligations, or take corrective action.

The project cannot guarantee complete confidentiality, but it will avoid unnecessary disclosure.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take actions including:

1. Requesting a correction
2. Issuing a private or public warning
3. Removing or editing content
4. Locking a conversation
5. Restricting participation
6. Temporarily suspending access
7. Permanently banning a participant
8. Referring credible threats or unlawful conduct to relevant authorities

The response should be proportionate to the behavior, its impact, prior conduct, and risk of recurrence.

## Conflicts of Interest

A maintainer involved in a complaint should not make the final enforcement decision when another qualified maintainer is available.

When the Project Lead is involved in a complaint, another maintainer or an independent reviewer should handle the matter where practical.

## Appeals

A participant may request reconsideration by replying privately to the enforcement notice.

An appeal should explain:

* Which decision is being challenged
* Why the decision may be incorrect or disproportionate
* Any relevant new information

Appeals do not pause urgent safety measures.

## No Retaliation

Retaliation against someone for reporting a concern, participating in a review, or supporting an affected person is prohibited.

## Good-Faith Reports

A report made in good faith will not result in action against the reporter merely because the concern is not substantiated.

Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves violate this Code of Conduct.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is informed by common open-source community standards and has been adapted for a project involving creative work, evaluation methodology, and public collaboration.
